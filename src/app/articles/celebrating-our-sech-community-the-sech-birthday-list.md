In the life of our church community, birthdays are more than dates on a calendar—they are moments to give thanks for God’s gifts and to reaffirm the bonds that hold us together. This article accompanies the forwarded message about the SECH Birthday List and explains how the list supports worship, fellowship, and pastoral care within our congregation.

## About the SECH Birthday List
The SECH Birthday List is a respectful directory used to acknowledge and celebrate the birthdays of members and friends within our church family. By recognizing these milestones, we acknowledge the ongoing presence of God’s work in each person’s life and create opportunities for prayer, encouragement, and connection.

## How the list informs our worship and fellowship
- Birthdays are highlighted in weekly announcements and at appropriate moments during worship, inviting the congregation to join in blessing those celebrating.
- The list supports pastoral care teams in planning outreach and visitations for members who may be away from the church building.
- It serves as a resource for planning special birthday blessings, cards, or small-group reminders throughout the month.
- The information helps determine seasonal or monthly outreach efforts that honor our community’s diversity and shared faith journey.

## Privacy and consent
We publish only information that individuals have consented to share publicly. The SECH Birthday List is handled with care and discretion to protect privacy while fostering a sense of belonging and care within the church.

## How to contribute or update birthdays
- To add a birthday or make updates, please contact the church office or use the church website’s submission form. We typically use month and day for public celebration, and we respect preferences for sharing or not sharing certain details.
- When submitting, include the name and preferred birthday information, and note any privacy considerations or special requests.
- If you already appear on the list and need changes, please request an update so the record remains accurate.

## Accessing the attached SECH Birthday List
The attached PDF, SECH Birthday List.pdf, contains the official current-year directory. If you require a corrected version or have questions about specific entries, contact the church office for assistance. The attachment is intended for authorized readers and members.

## Prayer and blessing
As we celebrate each birthday, let us lift up one another in prayer, asking God to bless and guide our community in the year ahead. May our gatherings be filled with joy, gratitude, and generous acts of care toward neighbors near and far.

## Closing
Thank you for being a vital part of the SECH family. Your presence enriches our worship, fellowship, and shared mission to live out God’s love in our community.
