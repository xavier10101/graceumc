## February Newsletter: Words of Grace and Community

We are excited to share the February issue of our church newsletter, highlighting compassionate living, worship, and service. This month's feature is the devotional guide, Words of Grace, designed to accompany your daily reflections as we journey through February together.

### Featured Devotional: Words of Grace

This month’s devotional, Words of Grace, invites you to pause, reflect, and respond to God’s grace in daily moments. The publication is available as a downloadable PDF:

### February Highlights

- Weekly worship services and congregational gatherings (see church calendar for times).
- Bible study and prayer groups forming for all ages.
- Family and youth ministries focusing on grace-filled discipleship.
- Outreach and service opportunities to support our neighbors in need.

### How to Engage

- Attend in-person or online worship each Sunday.
- Join a small group or volunteer team.
- Submit prayer requests through the church website or contact the ministries office.
- Subscribe to the digital newsletter for monthly updates.

### Scripture of the Month

> “Let the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, and the love of God, and the fellowship of the Holy Spirit be with you all.” — 2 Corinthians 13:14

### Closing Thoughts

As we walk through February, may we embody grace in our words and actions, building a community where all are welcomed, cared for, and inspired to live out their faith with hope. Thank you for being a vital part of our church family.

For more details, download Words of Grace 2026-FEB.pdf and stay tuned for upcoming events and opportunities to serve.
